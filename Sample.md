# The shape of a wave

A string vibrates around equilibrium. At position $x$ and time $t$, its displacement can be written as $y(x,t) = A\sin(kx-\omega t)$.

Measuring across a full cycle gives the **root mean square** amplitude.

$$
y_{\mathrm{rms}}
= \sqrt{\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} A^2\sin^2(\theta)\,d\theta}
= \frac{A}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

- $A$ is the peak amplitude.
- $k$ sets the spatial period.
- $\omega$ sets how quickly the phase changes.
