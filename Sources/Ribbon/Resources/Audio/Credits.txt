Ribbon typing sounds

Soft and Classic use edited excerpts of the following CC0 1.0 recordings:
- _stubb, “Typewriter keys_slow_near_mono.wav” (keys and the short taps used for Backspace)
  https://freesound.org/people/_stubb/sounds/406241/
- ikomlino, “Olimpia Typewriter typing1 spaces” (Space)
  https://freesound.org/people/ikomlino/sounds/852747/
- knufds, “Typewriter bell & carriage reset” (Return)
  https://freesound.org/people/knufds/sounds/345955/

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original uses Ribbon's synthesized audio. Its generator is in Support/generate-sounds.py.

The machine-specific presets each draw from recordings of the named typewriter.
Clips are cut, faded at the edges, and level-matched, preserving their original
pitch. Where an action was unlabeled, its clip is a nearby tap or mechanism
sound from that same machine. Olympia has a separately recorded Space key.

Olympia — ikomlino, vintage Olimpia recordings (CC0 1.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/ikomlino/packs/45600/
  Typing: 852748; Space: 852747; clicks: 852744; mechanism: 852750.

  https://freesound.org/people/facemusic/sounds/39223/

Smith-Corona Corsair — megashroom, “Typewriter 170428_1482.wav” (CC0 1.0)

IBM Selectric II — secretmojo, “Typewriter, IBM Selectric II” (CC0 1.0)
  https://freesound.org/people/secretmojo/sounds/224012/

The mechanical keyboard presets use trimmed, level-matched keystrokes.
Special keys use similar taps from the same recording where the source
does not identify individual keys.
